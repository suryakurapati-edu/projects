# projects
End-to-End projects in Data Engineering, Analytics, Science and AI-ML stream


I.	Big Data Analytics pipeline powered by Spark, AWS Cloud Services and Tableau:
As a Data Engineer (cloud) - Developed a scalable big data analytics pipeline on AWS EMR 7.9.0 (3-node m5.xlarge cluster, Hadoop 3.4.1, Spark 3.5.5) using custom PySpark framework for ETL, Amazon S3 (Parquet) for storage, and AWS RDS MySQL as the consumption layer, visualized in Tableau. The pipeline processes structured and semi-structured datasets (job listings, industries, skills, ZIP-state mappings) using PySpark framework deployed on AWS EMR to optimize LinkedIn’s job conversion, sponsorship, and employer retention. Tableau Dashboard insights identified contract jobs outperforming full-time roles, New York as a high-engagement hub, and key employers for sponsorship upselling or churn intervention.

II.	Big Data Healthcare Insurance Claims processing for Policy Renewal Dashboards and Fraud Claims Detection:
As a Data Engineer - Leveraged Big Data: Hadoop, Spark, AWS, Airflow, Tableau, Regression & Clustering to build a robust, reliable and scalable data engineering pipeline that processes 10-20 TB of claims data daily in 4 hrs with limited resources and utilize for downstream descriptive and predictive analytics.

III.	ConsultIQ – AI Assistant for Patients and Prescribers to revisit their visit:
As an AI Engineer - Patient and Prescriber hospital visit recorded as “.wav” audio file converted into transcription using “wav2vec2-base-960h” model, later break down into chunks using “langchain” and convert into embeddings using “all-MiniLM-L6-v2” and store into FAISS vector DB, and finally use “gemini-2.0-flash” LLM model with prompt engineering to generate response to user query.

IV.	Business Intelligence Business Analytics - Optimizing LinkedIn B2B sales outreach:
As a Data and Business Analyst - Leveraging LinkedIn’s job activity signals data such as posting frequency, sponsorship rate, salary benchmarks, and hiring trends, built a lead scoring model that prioritizes high-value employer accounts, enabling sales team to target top prospects, tailor outreach strategies, drive improved conversion rates with derived KPIs like lead engagement rate, conversion ratio, revenue growth from scored accounts through Tableau analytical dashboards and Salesforce CRM.

V.	Physician Targeting and Segmentation:
As a Data Scientist – Pharmaceutical company to optimize the launch of a new drug by predicting high-potential HCPs through headroom analysis (likelihood of a prescriber to prescribe new drug – Logistic Regression, Gradient Boost Classifier) and Segmentation (cluster prescribers into 4 Tiers  - K-Mean clustering) by taking HCP demographics, Competitor drug Rx data, Influential metrics, Call Sampling receive rate data into consideration resulting into optimized call plan, and increased marketing ROI.
